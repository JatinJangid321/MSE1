My Favorite Place - Modinagar

Modinagar is my favorite place in Uttar Pradesh. It is a beautiful and peaceful city located between Delhi and Meerut. Modinagar is known for its industrial history and the Modi Group. It is also well connected with nearby cities.

About the Place

Modinagar is a city in Ghaziabad district, Uttar Pradesh. It was founded by industrialist Rai Bahadur Gujar Mal Modi in the 1930s. The city developed around the Modi industrial group and is known for its historical and industrial importance.
Modinagar is also well connected to Delhi, Meerut and Ghaziabad. 

Location       : Ghaziabad District, Uttar Pradesh
Best Time      : October to March
Famous For     : Modi Group and Sugar Industry
Nearby Cities  : Delhi, Meerut and Ghaziabad

Attractions and Features

  • Modi Temple
  • Historical industrial heritage
  • Good connectivity to Delhi and Meerut
  • Local markets and food

Activities to Do

  1. Visit local historical places
  2. Explore the local markets
  3. Visit nearby temples and attractions
